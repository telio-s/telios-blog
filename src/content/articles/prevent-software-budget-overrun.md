Here are three simple ways to keep your budget from getting out of control when hiring someone to build software.

Before hiring or contacting a developer, try writing down your requirements first.

Writing down what you want helps you understand the goal and see the software more clearly.

Many business owners know they want software but still cannot answer what problem it should solve or which business rules should control how it behaves and displays information.

Here are three things worth writing down.

## 1. Who will use the software?

Be clear about who the users are and what the software should allow them to do.

List the features you want. For example, the software may need a booking system and a payment system, or customers may need to book and pay immediately after confirming a reservation.

## 2. Write the normal usage flow

Once you have listed the features or described what the software should do, arrange those actions into a sequence.

For example:

Book → Choose a date → Choose a time → Enter the number of guests → See available tables → Choose a table → Confirm the booking → See a payment QR code → See the successful payment screen

## 3. Identify the conditions the system needs to know

Read through the flow and identify the conditions or business rules hidden inside it.

For example:

- Customers can choose a date no more than 30 days in advance.
- If a customer is more than 15 minutes late, the system cancels the booking automatically.

These three steps already make the idea clearer and help the developer and business owner build a shared understanding before development begins.
